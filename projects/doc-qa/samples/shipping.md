# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the EU and 7-12 business days to the US and Canada. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days within the EU.

## Costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 EUR; otherwise it costs 4.90 EUR. Express shipping costs 14.90 EUR.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse, usually within 24 hours of the order.
