# Returns policy

## Return window
Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Sale items can be returned within 14 days for store credit only.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Shipping costs are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged.

## Exceptions
Personalised items, gift cards and opened software cannot be returned.
